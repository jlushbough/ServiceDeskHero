Desk field guide

Look things up while you work. Training does not pause the queue; use Pause if you need a break.

INC, REQ, Projects and KTLO

INC restores broken services. REQ handles the current shift’s layout, playback and print-setting requests. Projects tests and releases changes. KTLO means “keep the lights on”: prevent a known risk before it becomes an incident.

Acknowledge, hold and resolve

Sev 3 has no deadline until you acknowledge it, then 15 minutes. Sev 2 has 3 minutes from report; earned Sev 1 has 60 seconds. Hold organizes your work; it never pauses or resets an SLA. Resume a held case before acting. Resolved keeps the facts and outcome, including missed SLAs.

Diagnose without guessing

The caller’s claim may be wrong. Ask specific questions or run diagnostics to collect all the information needed. Leading questions and unrelated preferences waste time, but never lock out useful evidence. A correct first try earns +25 for direct diagnosis or useful evidence, once per stage; you can still finish the shift by learning as you go.

Prevent the next incident

Test a project for 18 seconds, then release the verified change for +450. An untested release creates a 60-second risk; use the KTLO rollback/test task before impact to avoid the incident; self-created risks earn no prevention bonus. These tasks share your time with tickets. Interrupt unfinished project work when an urgent issue arrives. Safely deferring an unreleased project earns no points and creates no risk.

Workarounds, bosses and shortcuts

A workaround returns in 11 seconds with its original SLA. Boss stages share one deadline. A successful Faker bluff buys 10 seconds but does not fix the fault. A acknowledges; 1–3 chooses an answer; Q/E changes the visible case; P pauses. Arrow keys move between workstream tabs.
